title: Homepage rebuilt around The Life of a Project
description: The landing now follows one project through its life, from possibility into presence and continuation, with dedicated philosophy, product, and roadmap pages.

publishedAt: 2026-08-31
lang: en
---

The homepage is no longer organised as feature categories. It now follows one
conceptual project through its life: possibility, distance, threshold, journey,
clarity, continuation, openness, and a closing question.

Its illustrations are software artifacts developers already recognise: a
repository, a localhost address, deployment stages, a diagnostic artifact when a
health check fails, and the lineage of a work. They all describe the same
project rather than a separate illustration per section.

Colour now carries narrative meaning. The page opens on a neutral surface, teal
enters exactly at the Threshold chapter, then grows confident once the work
becomes reachable. Dark surfaces appear only twice, and both times because the
content demands it: the Threshold as the single dramatic transition, and
Clarity as a diagnostic surface.

Content that used to pile up on the homepage moved to its own pages. The seven
principles and the meaning of the tagline now live on Philosophy, capabilities
and their status on Product, and horizons on Roadmap. The about and governance
pages merged into a single Open Source page, with the old URLs redirected.

Motion is used only when something is becoming, and only one thing at a time.
Deployment stages light up in sequence because that is genuinely a process. The
whole story holds with motion disabled and with JavaScript switched off.
