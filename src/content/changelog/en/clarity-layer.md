title: A clarity layer over the existing narrative
description: The hero gains a concrete explanation and a next step, user personas appear on the homepage, and following the progress becomes the single primary action.
version: "0.6.0"
publishedAt: 2026-09-09
lang: en
---

Four independent reviews assessed the homepage and reached the same
conclusion: the narrative is strong, but a fast-scanning visitor waits too
long to learn what Sakala is. This update closes that gap without changing
the identity.

The hero still opens with the same line, now followed by one plain sentence
and two next steps: follow the progress, or read the documentation. The hero
previously offered no action at all.

The homepage gains a chapter that answers directly who Sakala is built for:
students and interns, lecturers and mentors, beginner developers and learning
communities — taken from the documented personas, not invented.

The question "which stacks can I deploy?" is answered honestly through a
repository-analysis artifact: the stack is recognized from your own
Dockerfile or through Railpack, and the reading can be inspected before the
build runs. There is no framework list, because no list has ever been
promised.

The closing section is now a single follow block: GitHub, the changelog RSS,
and the roadmap. The main navigation puts Product and Documentation first,
with Philosophy still one click away for those who seek it.
