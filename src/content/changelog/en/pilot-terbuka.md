---
title: The Sakala pilot is open to anyone
description: Anyone can now sign up in the Console and use hosted Sakala at no cost during the pilot, within limits on projects, containers, and builds per hour.
publishedAt: 2026-11-19
lang: en
---

From today, registration in the Console is open to anyone. Hosted Sakala is
still in its pilot, and during the pilot it can be used at no cost, within limits on projects, containers, and builds per
hour, and projects sleep after 30 minutes without visitors. There is no uptime
guarantee yet, so Sakala is not a fit for important applications. All the
limits are in [Pilot Limits](/en/docs/batas-pilot).

If you want to try it, start with [Your First Deploy](/en/docs/deploy-pertama).
Feedback during the pilot can be sent through the **Kirim Masukan** (send
feedback) menu in the Console.

Still in progress: limiting outgoing traffic from user containers, basic
per-project metrics, and the single-node self-host installer in the
[`gmedia/sakala`](https://github.com/gmedia/sakala) repository, which is tested
on clean machines but not released yet. Its status is kept up to date on the
[Roadmap](/en/roadmap).
