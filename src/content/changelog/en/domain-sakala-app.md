title: Applications move to sakala.app
description: Applications deployed through Sakala now live at <slug>.sakala.app, a domain deliberately kept apart from sakala.dev.
publishedAt: 2026-11-18
lang: en
---

Applications deployed through Sakala now live at `<slug>.sakala.app`, replacing
`*.run.sakala.dev`. Existing projects were moved and redeployed, and the old
addresses no longer work.

The application domain is kept apart from `sakala.dev` on purpose, so user
content does not share reputation or cookies with the Sakala site and Console.
The reasons are in the
[FAQ](/en/docs/faq#why-do-applications-live-on-sakalaapp-instead-of-sakaladev)
and [ADR-016](/en/docs/proyek/adr).
