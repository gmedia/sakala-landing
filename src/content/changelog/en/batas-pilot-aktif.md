Terms of Service §3 used to say that automatic sleep and the build limit per
hour were not active yet, and that both would be announced before they were
turned on. This entry is that announcement. Sleep has applied since 13 November
2026, and the build limit per hour since 18 November 2026.

The limits for a personal workspace without a custom limit profile:

- projects sleep after 30 minutes without visitors, then start again when their
  address is opened or when you wake them from the Console;
- 20 builds per workspace per hour, counted over the last 60 minutes;
- 3 projects and 3 running containers per workspace.

Team workspaces and workspaces with an admin limit profile can have different
numbers. [Terms of Service](/en/syarat) §3 has been updated with these numbers,
without changing its version or effective date, because what changed are
operational limits, not your rights and obligations. All the limits are in
[Pilot Limits](/en/docs/batas-pilot).
