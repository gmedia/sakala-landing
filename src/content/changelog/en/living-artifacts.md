---

A full audit of the previous homepage found two things: every artifact stood
still, and the motion budget was spent on text. This update reverses both
without changing the story.

Nine chapters become six. Distance folds into the hero as a single two-state
artifact: repository on the left, localhost on the right, and still no
address anyone else can open. The personas sit directly under the plain
sentence in the Threshold chapter. The closing joins the Open chapter and
carries the three latest entries from this changelog as proof that the
project is moving.

The deployment now runs. Five named stages complete one by one, the next one
lights up, and the public address appears at the end. Without JavaScript or
with reduced motion, everything is already finished from the start. The mark
in the Threshold chapter draws itself in two strokes: source, then presence.
Text no longer fades in; only artifacts become.

The artifacts borrow the grammar of the Console Wave 1 design: status banner,
meta strip, stage timeline, log panel, analysis card, and the "your project is
live" moment. Stage names follow the Console. All of it is labelled as design,
not screenshots of a running product. The Product page gains the Dashboard's
project cards with the same label.

The Open Source page replaces the promise of a contributor list with three
contribution paths that are open today. Every main page now has its own
social preview, drawn from the site's fonts without a browser.
