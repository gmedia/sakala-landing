---
title: Projects sleep when idle and wake when visited
description: Projects without visitors for 30 minutes are now put to sleep, then started again when their address is opened, so the pilot's capacity can be shared.
publishedAt: 2026-11-13
lang: en
---

Projects with no visitors for 30 minutes are now put to sleep. The address
stays the same. The next visitor sees the **Proyek sedang dinyalakan** (project
is starting) page, and the application opens by itself once it is ready. Owners
can also wake it from the Console, which marks sleeping projects with a
**Tidur** (sleeping) label.

Sleep lets the pilot's capacity be shared by more people, and it is one of the
conditions before registration opens to everyone
([ADR-015](/en/docs/proyek/adr)). The numbers and what they mean for your
application are in [Pilot Limits](/en/docs/batas-pilot).
