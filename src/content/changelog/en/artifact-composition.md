---
title: Artifacts deepened, and the docs stop promising what does not exist
description: The homepage illustrations were rebuilt so their construction carries the meaning, and documentation pages that promised usage were withdrawn while Sakala is not yet available.
version: "0.5.0"
publishedAt: 2026-08-31
lang: en
---

The homepage illustrations were accurate but flat: one browser window to
explain a project that cannot get out, two separate cards to explain cause and
effect, and a vertical list to explain branching. Each of them leaned on its
caption to carry the point.

Now the construction does the work. A project running locally appears as a
window floating at the centre of a gridded field, held inside a frame. The
Threshold became a chapter-wide crossing, with a horizontal line at the exact
point where the crossing happens. Deployment stages and their result moved into
a single frame, so cause and effect are visible together. Continuation became a
graph that genuinely branches and then converges again.

The green line now carries across chapter boundaries rather than stopping
inside individual artifacts, so moving between chapters reads as one journey.

The 404 and changelog pages followed into the same language. Both had been left
behind on the older arrangement.

The documentation changed more sharply. Sakala is not available as a public
service, yet some pages were written as though the flow could be run today.
Pages that promised usage were withdrawn, and what remains is framed as design
documents: explanations of how things work and where they are heading, not
guides to using something that does not exist. Old addresses redirect to the
documentation overview.
